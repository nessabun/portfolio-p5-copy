Jennesa Mecano is a mixed-media artist based in San Jose, California. Working with traditional and digital media with the occasional handmade craft, her pieces explore the art of deep, implicit meanings that can be left to the audience’s interpretations. Her technique primarily focuses on making pencil drawings and digital illustrations. Her creative process is driven by her love for storytelling and visual expression. What originally began as a leisurely hobby in her freetime, Jennesa is now currently working towards earning her Bachelor’s degree in Digital Media Arts in San Jose State University. She currently aspires to build up a small business selling her art in the form of fun trinkets and create games with indie developers.
